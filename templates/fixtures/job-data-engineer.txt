Senior Data Engineer, Payments Platform
Lumen Payments (fictional company, test fixture)
London, UK (hybrid, 2 days a week in the office)

About the role
Lumen Payments moves money for small businesses across the UK and West Africa. Our data platform
team builds the pipelines that feed fraud detection, finance reporting and the analytics our product
teams use every day. We are looking for a senior data engineer to own core batch and streaming
pipelines end to end.

What you will do
- Design, build and run batch pipelines in Spark and streaming pipelines on Kafka.
- Own our dbt project and help analysts write models they can maintain themselves.
- Orchestrate workflows with Airflow and keep them observable and reliable.
- Work with the fraud team on low-latency features for real-time scoring.
- Reduce cloud spend on AWS without slowing anyone down.
- Mentor engineers and review designs across the team.

What we are looking for
Must have:
- 5 or more years building data pipelines in production.
- Strong Python and SQL.
- Hands-on experience with Spark, Kafka and Airflow.
- Experience with a cloud data warehouse.
- Experience in financial services or payments.

Nice to have:
- dbt.
- Snowflake.
- Kubernetes.
- Data contracts or schema registries.
- Experience leading a small team.

What we offer
Competitive salary, share options, 30 days of holiday and a learning budget.
